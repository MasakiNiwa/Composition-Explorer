# References

Composition Explorer draws on the following research and classical theory.
Only **ideas** are referenced — no code, images, models or datasets from these
works are included. All algorithms are implemented independently.
(The same list is shown on the in-app About page: `src/lib/references.ts`.)

## Research

- L. Liu, R. Chen, L. Wolf, D. Cohen-Or. **Optimizing Photo Composition.**
  Computer Graphics Forum (Eurographics 2010). https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8659.2009.01645.x
  — aesthetic score functions and composition optimization.
- **Can Machines Understand Composition? (PICD), CVPR 2025.**
  — classification and understanding of photographic composition types.
  The PICD dataset is **not** used or redistributed.
- K. Deb, A. Pratap, S. Agarwal, T. Meyarivan. **A fast and elitist multiobjective
  genetic algorithm: NSGA-II.** IEEE TEC 6(2), 2002. https://doi.org/10.1109/4235.996017
  — planned for multi-objective exploration (Phase 4).

## Art and geometry

- Rule of thirds — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_thirds
- Golden ratio, golden section, golden spiral — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_ratio
- Linear perspective and vanishing points — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perspective_(graphical)
- Horizon / eye-level rule — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizon
- R. Arnheim. *Art and Visual Perception: A Psychology of the Creative Eye* (1954, rev. 1974)
  — visual weight and balance.

## Policy

- Research ideas are re-implemented from scratch; papers are cited here.
- External code, images, models or datasets are only added after checking
  their license individually (no non-commercial-only or unlicensed material).
- Third-party software licenses are tracked in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
